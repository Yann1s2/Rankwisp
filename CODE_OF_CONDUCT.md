# Community code of conduct

Rankwisp welcomes people of every background and experience level. Be considerate,
assume good faith and keep feedback specific to the work. Questions and corrections
are welcome; harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, doxxing
and unwanted solicitation are not.

Respect privacy. Do not post someone else's personal information, private app data or
credentials. Avoid spam, purchased engagement and demands for stars or contributions.
Participation is voluntary.

These expectations apply to the project's issues, pull requests and other spaces
maintained by the project. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close a
thread or restrict participation, taking context and severity into account.

Report concerns privately to **info@zhstudio.ch**. Include enough context to explain
what happened without publishing sensitive information. Reports will be handled with
discretion; no guaranteed response time is promised. Security vulnerabilities should
follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
